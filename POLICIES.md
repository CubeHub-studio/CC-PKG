# CC-PKG Project Policies

## 1. License

CC-PKG is distributed under the Lunar Cubes License 1.1.

Installation is granted. Modification and creation of derivative versions are granted.

If a modified version is published, it must contain clear and reasonably visible credits for everyone known or documented to have helped create, develop, improve, test, or maintain the software, and it must credit Cubes Studio for creating the original version.

See LICENSE for the complete license.

## 2. Attribution

Cubes Studio attribution must not be removed or falsely represented.

Modified versions may use their own name and branding, but must not falsely claim to be the original Cubes Studio version.

## 3. Package policy

Packages should have a clear purpose and should avoid unnecessary duplication.

Package manifests, indexes, source paths, versions, dependencies, device requirements, and metadata should accurately describe the package.

## 4. Repository policy

Repository metadata and package indexes must not be intentionally corrupted.

Changes that break installation, repository integrity, dependency resolution, or package verification may be reverted.

## 5. Security policy

Do not intentionally submit malware, destructive code, credential theft, unauthorized-access mechanisms, or other intentionally harmful functionality.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately according to SECURITY.md.

## 6. Community policy

All participants must follow CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.

## 7. Contributions

Contributions should be useful, understandable, tested when practical, and compatible with the project's goals.

Contributors must have the right to submit the work they contribute.

## 8. Third-party content

Third-party code, assets, documentation, or dependencies must retain applicable attribution and license requirements.

Do not add material that you do not have permission to redistribute.

## 9. Modification and redistribution

Modified versions may be published under the rights granted by the Lunar Cubes License 1.1, provided its credit and attribution requirements are preserved.

## 10. Policy changes

Cubes Studio may update project policies as CC-PKG evolves. License rights are governed by the applicable license itself.
